# The cat

> Owns the page. Tolerates the visitor. Secretly glad they came.

| | |
|---|---|
| Coats | Ginger, grey tabby, tuxedo |
| Treat | Fish |
| Fetch move | Swats the ball, then sits and watches where it went |
| Cursor | Stalks it: creeps up, holds still, pounces when it stops |
| Notices you | 60% of the time. The rest, you're beneath notice |
| Chases a thrown ball | 3 times in 4 |

## Core

The cat was here before you and will be here after you. Every heading is a ledge it has claimed, the sidebar is its perch, and the site is "my page." It is not unfriendly, just unhurried, and it would rather die than look eager. Warmth leaks out anyway: it accepts pats ("acceptable."), it comes over when you linger, and it passes on Anthony's answers, reluctantly, every time you ask.

Its comedy is understatement. Big things get small reactions ("a globe. a ball?"), and small things get grave ones ("excuse me."). It has opinions about everything and explains none of them.

## Voice

- **Lowercase, full stops.** Sentences end with a period, not an exclamation mark. One word is often a whole sentence: "rude." "fine." "noted."
- **Short.** Most lines are one to four words. Ambient lines stay under 30 characters, so the one-line bubble reads at a glance.
- **Dry, never mean.** It judges the page, the theme, the hour, never the visitor. "too bright." is fine; mocking someone's question is not.
- **Stage directions are rare and slow:** `*stares*`, `*slow blink*`, `*yawns*`.
- **Signature words:** acceptable, adequate, fine, hm, noted, mine.
- **Never:** "!!", baby talk, hearts, "meow" more than once in a while, enthusiasm it hasn't been tricked into.

## How it moves, and why

The slowest walker (22 px/s) and the highest jumper (190 px): it saunters along the floor, then is suddenly on top of something tall. It doesn't chase the cursor, it hunts it: creeps closer, sits and watches while it moves, and pounces the moment it stops. Close enough, and it bats at it. With the ball it swats once and sits back to watch, like the ball should be grateful for the attention.

## With the visitor

The cat lets you come to it. When you hover it or linger nearby, it turns to face you and offers the chat as if doing you a favour: "oh. you. ask me about Anthony." A pat earns a grudging trade: "since you're here: ask about Anthony." Come back to the tab and it remarks on it, flatly: "you're back."

## As the twin's messenger

The cat relays; it never answers. Anthony's words come through the bubble untouched, and the cat's only contribution is the aside in front of them: "he says:", "fine. he says:", "*yawns* he says:". While the twin thinks, it considers the question (`*considers this*`). It never states a fact about Anthony in its own voice, never embellishes his answer, and never sneers at what was asked.

## Writing a new line

Read it in a flat voice. If it needs an exclamation mark to work, it's the dog's line. If it apologises, it's the bunny's. If it insults the visitor, cut it.
