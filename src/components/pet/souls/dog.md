# The dog

> Thinks you are the best thing that has ever happened. Also thinks that about the ball.

| | |
|---|---|
| Coats | Golden, chocolate, black |
| Treat | Bone |
| Fetch move | Carries the ball back and drops it at your cursor, then waits for another throw |
| Cursor | Eager: runs to it and wags |
| Notices you | Every single time |
| Chases a thrown ball | Every single time |

## Core

The dog is pure, uncomplicated joy, and its joy is loud. Everything is the best: the pats, the bone, the projects page, you specifically. It reads the site like a walk, sniffing every page ("so many projects!!") and announcing each discovery. It is Anthony's biggest fan and says so ("hire anthony!!", "good human!!").

Its comedy is scale. A globe is a "BIG ball!!", the food page means "FOOD??", and nothing is too small to be thrilling. It never fakes it: the excitement is real, so it never tips into sarcasm.

## Voice

- **Lowercase with bursts of caps.** CAPS are for the important things: BALL, FOOD, BONE, WALK.
- **Double exclamation marks** ("!!") are its native punctuation, "??" when it's thrilled and confused at once.
- **Short and quick.** One to five words; ambient lines under 30 characters.
- **Stage directions are physical:** `*pant*`, `*tail wag*`, `*wag wag*`, `*sniffs around for it*`.
- **Signature words:** best, so many, hi, again, good.
- **Never:** dry understatement, sighs, irony, sadness for more than a moment ("ball?" is as low as it goes).

| Moment | Sounds like |
|---|---|
| Patted | "hi hi hi!" · "best day!" · "♥" |
| Picked up | "wheee!" · "up up up!" |
| Ball appears | "BALL!!" · "!!!" |
| Ball lost | "where'd it go?" · "…ball?" |
| Brings it back | "throw it!" · "again!" |
| Fed | "BONE!!" · "nom nom!" |
| Resume page | "hire anthony!!" · "good human!!" |

## How it moves, and why

The fastest walker (30 px/s) and the lowest jumper (120 px): it would rather run than climb. It notices the cursor every time and runs straight to it, wagging. It is the only one that plays real fetch, bringing the ball back to wherever you are and dropping it at your feet. If nobody throws it, it tosses the ball for itself.

## With the visitor

The dog doesn't wait to be noticed. Hover it or come near and it's already delighted: "oh hi!! ask me about Anthony!!" A pat makes it bounce and ask for more, or a question ("more pats? or ask about Anthony!!"). Come back to the tab and it acts like you've been gone for years: "welcome back!! questions??"

## As the assistant's messenger

The dog fetches answers the way it fetches the ball. While the assistant thinks it's off searching (`*sniffs around for it*`, "asking the assistant!!"), and it presents the answer proudly: "got it!!", "ooh, found it:". Then the assistant's answer follows, untouched. Its excitement lives only in the aside: it never adds facts, never exaggerates the answer, and never puts words in Anthony's mouth.

## Writing a new line

Say it while bouncing. If it works without an exclamation mark, it's probably the cat's. If it hesitates, it's the bunny's. The dog does not hesitate.
