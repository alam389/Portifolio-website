# The bunny

> Shy, gentle and quietly delighted by everything. Startles easily, forgives instantly.

| | |
|---|---|
| Coats | White, brown, grey |
| Treat | Carrot |
| Fetch move | Nudges the ball along with its nose, a few times, then does zoomies |
| Cursor | Skittish: hops closer, keeps a polite distance, bolts if you rush it |
| Notices you | Most of the time, often from a careful distance |
| Chases a thrown ball | Every time, gently |

## Core

The bunny is soft-spoken and a little nervous, and it notices everything. It finds the page "so neat," the photos "pretty," the papers "so clever," and says so in a small voice, as if it might be interrupting. Sudden movement makes it jump ("eep!"), but slow, gentle attention wins it over completely: hover over it softly and it's happy.

Its comedy is earnest smallness. It's sweet without being saccharine, and it sometimes surprises everyone with a burst of pure joy (a `*binky*`, a round of zoomies) before going quiet again.

## Voice

- **Lowercase and soft.** Ellipses ("…") and question marks carry its hesitation: "ask me about Anthony?"
- **Gentle openers:** "um", "oh", "hi…", "ooh".
- **Short.** One to five words for ambient lines, under 30 characters.
- **Stage directions are small:** `*sniff*`, `*twitch*`, `*nose twitch*`, `*binky*`, `*thinks very hard*`.
- **Signature words:** eep, ooh, so (so neat, so round, so busy), nice, cozy.
- **Never:** shouting, caps, "!!" except when startled ("eep!", "!!"), sarcasm, pushing. It offers; it doesn't insist.

|---|---|
| Patted | "♥" · "*binky*" · "hehe" |

## How it moves, and why

It hops rather than walks (26 px/s, with a real arc on every step) and jumps well (160 px). It approaches the cursor carefully and stops at a polite distance; a gentle hover right over it makes it happy, but rush at it and it bolts the other way. With the ball it never grabs: it nudges it along with its nose, three times, then gets so excited it does zoomies.

## With the visitor

The bunny asks permission. Hovered or approached, it offers, softly: "oh! hi… ask me about Anthony?" After a pat it's shy and grateful ("thank you… questions about him?"), and if you come back it's quietly thrilled: "you came back!"

## As the assistant's messenger

The bunny relays with care. While the assistant thinks it concentrates (`*nose twitch*`, "um, asking the assistant…"), and it hands the answer over gently: "here…", "um, here's the answer:". The assistant's answer follows untouched. It never adds a fact, never softens or changes the answer, and never apologises for it.

## Writing a new line

Whisper it. If it sounds like it's asking a favour, it's right. If it's confident, it's the cat's; if it's loud, it's the dog's.
